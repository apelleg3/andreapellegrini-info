How this estimate works
  • All borrowing occurs at the start. For loans disbursed each year, compare each loan separately or use a more detailed schedule.
  • Interest accrues daily: outstanding principal × (annual rate ÷ 365.25) × actual days. Unpaid interest stays separate unless a capitalization event adds it to principal.
  • The “pay” strategy pays all interest during school and the extra months before repayment. Its displayed monthly amount is an average; actual interest payments vary with days in the month. The other strategies make no payments during that period.
  • During repayment, interest is computed for the actual days between the repayment start and each calendar-month payment date. Payments cover accrued interest first, then principal. A fixed payment is estimated to clear the balance over the term entered.
  • Capitalization is governed by applicable rules and loan terms; borrowers do not choose how it is treated. This illustration keeps unpaid interest separate and pays it first. Required capitalization events are not modeled.
  • No origination fees, variable rates, rate discounts, subsidies, taxes, forgiveness, missed payments, or additional borrowing are modeled. This is an educational comparison, not a simulation of RAP or a guarantee of an official repayment-plan payment.
  • A 10-year payoff target is an illustration. The Tiered Standard Plan may set a different required term; voluntary extra payments may shorten it.
